Wizard's Keep — Checkpoint 0.2.0 (Expanded)

What changed since 0.1.0:
- Visitor roster expanded to 14 characters with reputation gates.
  Early: normal villagers & peddlers.
  Mid: tinkerer, witch, quiet guest.
  Late: fungus herbalist, skeleton mage, young treant, ghost noble.
  Very late: void-touched client.
- Each visitor has specific request type + amount + reward.
- Tiny procedural visitor & monster sprites (12-18 px) instead of generic circles.
- Garden now has growth stages that advance with days.
- Better visual detail on every screen (tower flag, better cauldron, denser forest, etc.).
- Reputation now actively unlocks stranger clients and improves rewards.
- Night waves scale more interestingly; turrets and walls matter more.
- Journal reflects current reputation tier with flavor text.
- Header shows potions count.
- Starting resources slightly higher for better first-day feel.

Still placeholder art (procedural). Real 16x16 sprite atlases remain future work once packing rules are decided.
Core loop is now noticeably more alive and the revolving door feels real.
